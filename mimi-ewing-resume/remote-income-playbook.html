Part 3 · First 7 Days

  1. Day 1: Sign up for Respondent, User Interviews, dscout, and Prolific (30 minutes total). Apply to every caregiver/parenting study — this is the “paid today” lane.
  2. Day 2: Apply to LanguageLine and Propio as a Thai interpreter, and create an italki Community Tutor profile. These three run on her passport skill: Thai.
  3. Day 3: Apply to JustAnswer (parenting) and SitterStream; refresh UrbanSitter/Care.com profiles with a “virtual sitter · bilingual Thai” headline.
  4. Day 4: Draft the sleep-consulting offer: one page, three packages ($150 call / $350 full plan / $600 two-week support). Text her six former families for testimonials — she has a decade of them.
  5. Day 5: Submit an Outschool educator application with one class: Baby Sign Language for New Parents.
  6. Day 6: List two Fiverr gigs: allergy-safe weekly meal plan; toddler meal plan. Apply to Belay and Time etc as a household-manager-turned-VA.
  7. Day 7: Post the sleep-consulting offer in three local-parent and Thai-community Facebook groups. First consult usually comes from there, not from any platform.

Rate-card discipline: never price below $20/hr for live work — the resume supports it. Consulting is sold in packages, not hours. Platform fees (15–33%) are the cost of the first ten clients; after that, referrals are free.
