▚ FREQUENTLY ASKED

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Is Training Mode free?+

Yes — it's free to start. No card and no App Store needed.

Do I need to download it from the App Store?+

No. Training Mode runs right in your browser and installs to your home screen like a normal app (it's a PWA). No store, no approval wait, no download.

SHOW ME HOW ›

Do I need equipment or a gym?+

No. Most modes work with just bodyweight and shadowboxing. If you have a heavy bag, battle ropes, or weights, you can fold them in — but nothing is required.

What workouts are included?+

Fit Mode (workout builder, quick mission, programs, cardio), Fight Mode (training camp, just train, fight focus, combo coach, practice), Combat Conditioning circuits, and the Training Arcade sagas.

Does it work offline?+

Once installed to your home screen, core training works without a connection — handy for gyms with bad signal or training outdoors.

What is Training Camp?+

A 12-level fight camp: Foundation, Development, Hard Camp and Taper, ending in a Title Fight. Clear a level to unlock the next; from Development on, each level has a skill session and a conditioning session.

What is the Training Arcade?+

Themed 10-stage sagas where each stage is a real workout you fight through. You drain a boss's HP bar rep by rep, earn stars against time cutoffs, and climb to the next stage.

How do ranks and XP work?+

Every session earns XP. XP climbs your rank from Rookie to Champion, evolving your avatar along the way — with three secret tiers waiting past Champion.

Will there be a game?+

A Training Mode game is in the works. Your workout progress is designed to carry over — XP converts to in-game stats, and your unlocked avatar tiers appear as playable forms.

How do I join or give feedback?+

Tap Start Free to open the app — no form to fill out. To send feedback, use the Give Feedback link at the bottom of any page.
