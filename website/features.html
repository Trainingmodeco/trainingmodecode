HOW A SAGA WORKS

01PICK A SAGATap a poster. The intro card shows the protocol, its tagline and its rewards → START THE CLIMB.
02THE LADDERTen nodes up a spine. Locked nodes say CLEAR N, your current node pulses gold, cleared nodes show ✓ and their stars. The boss waits at the top.
03THE BRIEFINGStage title, difficulty pips, mission objectives, your best time, star goals → ENTER STAGE.
04PATH + DIFFICULTYFIT, FIGHT or FULL ARC (which paths depends on the saga) × EASY / NORMAL / HARD. Or CHALLENGE A FRIEND with a code or QR.
05THE SESSIONFIGHT path: round timer, coach-called round goals and combos, finishers. FIT path: counted sets and reps, the voice counts every one. FULL ARC: both, back to back.
06STAGE CLEAREvery round done and verified. Campaign sagas: stars = the difficulty you beat — ★ EASY · ★★ NORMAL · ★★★ HARD, +1 for FULL ARC (max 3). One Hundred: stars by time. Best time kept. XP banked, campaign badge earned, next node unlocks.
07THE BOSSStage 10. A mandatory Answer-the-Bell gate, a boss HP bar that drains per cleared round, double XP. Fight boss ≈ 9 rounds, ~39 min. Fit boss = 12-round burnout, ~30 min. BOSS DEFEATED.

SECTION 04 · TRAINING CAMP

TWELVE LEVELS TO A TITLE FIGHT.

Fight Mode's structured path. One camp, one fighting style, one Title Fight.

01THE LADDERTwelve levels, five phases. FOUNDATION 1–3 (Basics · Rhythm · Finisher) → DEVELOPMENT 4–6 (Base · Volume · Power) → HARD CAMP 7–9 (Engine · Grind · Peak) → TAPER 10–11 (Speed · Sharpen — volume cut, round length kept) → 12 TITLE FIGHT.
02SKILL + CONDITIONINGLevels 1–3 are single sessions. Levels 4–11 are splits: SESSION 1 · SKILL first, SESSION 2 · CONDITIONING. A level clears at ✓✓, or run FULL CAMP back to back.
03FIGHTING STYLESThree per discipline. Boxing: Pressure Fighter · Counter Fighter · Well-Rounded. Kickboxing: Pressure Fighter · Distance Kicker · Counter Fighter. Muay Thai: Technician · Clinch Fighter · Power Puncher. MMA: Wrestler · Striker · Brawler.
04EASY / NORMAL / HARDPass = 85 / 90 / 95% completion plus technique and the tactical objective. A readiness check runs before every session — GO, SUGGEST EASY (streak kept) or REST (no penalty).
05TITLE FIGHTOne extended session: 20–45 active minutes striking, up to 5 × 5:00 for MMA. Hard needs a passed readiness check. Clear it for the Camp Champion trophy.
