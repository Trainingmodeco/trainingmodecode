# ManyChat: comment → DM the app link

Instagram (and Facebook) captions end with **"Comment TRAIN"**. ManyChat
answers every TRAIN (or BETA, which the older scripts use) with a DM holding
the link. Free plan is enough to start.

## Set up the flow (once, in ManyChat)

1. **Automation → + New Automation**. If a template like "Auto-DM links from
   comments" is offered, use it; otherwise **Start from scratch**.
2. **Trigger:** Instagram → *User comments on your Post or Reel*.
   - Post: **Any Post or Reel** (if your plan only allows one, pick the reel
     you're promoting and repeat per reel).
   - Keywords (comment contains): `TRAIN`, `BETA`, `LINK`.
3. **Public reply** under the comment (turn on, add all three so they rotate):
   - Sent! Check your DMs 🥊
   - Link's in your DMs. Go clear a stage.
   - DM'd you. Hit CLEARED when you're done.
4. **Action: Send Message** (Instagram):

   > 🥊 Here's Training Mode: Fit, Fight and the Training Arcade in one app.
   > Pick a mode, clear your first stage.
   >
   > This month's drop is SHOTO, free until Nov 30.

   Add a **button**: "Open Training Mode" → `https://apptrainingmode.com`
5. **Set Live.** Test it: comment TRAIN on your own reel from another account.
6. Repeat for **Facebook** (trigger: *User comments on your Facebook post*) if
   you post Reels there too.

## Keep it current

The drop line changes with the app's concept drop (`brand/brand.json` →
`drops`): SHOTO until 2026-11-30, **ULTRA EGO** from 2026-12-01, FLOW STATE
from 2027-02-01. Update the DM text on the first of those months.

## Rules

- Never promise XP or rewards for commenting, following or sharing; the app
  rewards the workout only.
- TikTok and YouTube captions say "Link in bio" instead (ManyChat doesn't
  reply to comments there).
