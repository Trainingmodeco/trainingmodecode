Training Mode

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Training Mode (“we,” “us,” the “app”) is a combat-fitness training web app available at apptrainingmode.com. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. We keep this deliberately short because the app is built to keep your data on your own device wherever possible.

Information we handle

  • Account details (only if you sign in). Signing in is optional. If you choose “Continue with Google,” we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google so we can create and recognize your account. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Your training data lives on your device. Workouts, XP, levels, streaks, saved routines, custom combos, and app settings are stored locally in your browser (using localStorage) on the device you train with. If you are not signed in, none of it is uploaded to our servers.
  • Progress backup (only if you sign in). When you are signed in, the app keeps a copy of that training data in your account so you can restore it on a new phone or after clearing your browser. Only your account can read or change this copy.
  • Ghosts you share (only if you sign in, or send a link). Ghost challenges let you race a recorded session. Some of these recordings can be shared with other players, as explained in Ghost challenges and sharing below.
  • Subscription status (only if you subscribe). If you purchase Pro, payment is processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. We keep a record that your account is entitled to Pro so the app can unlock those features.
  • Basic, privacy-friendly analytics. We may use aggregate, cookieless analytics to understand how many people use each feature. These figures are anonymous and are not used to identify or profile individual users.

How we use it

  • To create your account and keep you signed in across visits.
  • To save your progress and preferences so the app works the way you left it.
  • To unlock the features included in your subscription.
  • To understand, in aggregate, which features are used so we can improve the app.
We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show third-party advertising in the app.

Ghost challenges and sharing

A ghost is a record of a finished Fight Focus session: how many strikes you threw and when (counted in 10-second slices), your totals, the number and length of rounds, the difficulty, the discipline, the date, and your avatar setting (male or female art). A ghost does not contain your email, your Google profile, or your location.

  • Anonymous ghosts for other players. If you are signed in, up to 2 of your Fight Focus ghosts each week are added to a pool that other players can be challenged by. Your name is removed on our servers before the ghost is stored. Other players see only “A Warrior,” your fighter level, and the session numbers above — never your name, email, photo, or account. This is on by default. Turn it off any time in Profile → Edit profile → Let others race my ghosts; turning it off deletes every ghost you have shared. Shared ghosts are offered to other players for up to 60 days and are kept until you turn sharing off or delete your account.
  • Haunt a Friend (only when you choose to send one). After a session you can send your ghost to a friend. If you are signed in, we store that ghost with a 6-character code; anyone who has the code or its link can open that session, including the display name you set in the app, for 7 days. After 7 days the code stops working. The stored ghost stays linked to your account until you delete your account. If you are not signed in, the link you send contains the session and your display name inside the link itself; it is not stored on our servers, and anyone who has the link can see it.
  • Ghosts you receive. A friend’s ghost or a stranger’s ghost that you open is saved on your device — and in your own progress backup if you are signed in. It is never passed on to anyone else.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of trusted providers to run the app:

  • Google — sign-in.
  • Supabase — stores your account record and, if you sign in, your progress backup and the ghosts you share.
  • Stripe — processes subscription payments.
  • Netlify — hosts and delivers the app.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy and only to the extent needed to provide its service to us.

Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without an account. Most training features work without signing in.
  • Sign out at any time to end your session on that device.
  • Delete your local training data by clearing your browser’s site data for apptrainingmode.com.
  • Stop sharing ghosts in Profile → Edit profile → Let others race my ghosts. Turning it off removes the ghosts you have already shared.
  • Access or delete your account data. Email us and we will help you access or permanently delete the account information associated with your sign-in. Deleting your account also deletes your progress backup, your shared ghosts, and your haunt codes.

Children

Training Mode is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data? Email trainingmode.co@gmail.com.
