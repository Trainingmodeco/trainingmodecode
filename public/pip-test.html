PiP FEASIBILITY TEST

Not part of the app — a throwaway page that answers one question: does a floating window keep updating after you leave the app? Android can only float a <video>, so the timer is drawn to a canvas and streamed into one. If the numbers freeze when you switch away, the web route is dead and the mini-player needs the native wrapper.
