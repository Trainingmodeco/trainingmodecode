# Flags

**1.** **MOD_UI_S04_FIGHT** (S4) has 4 of 5 generated rounds at `complexity: "single"`. Single rounds are exempt from the 60/40 variety rule, so the stage risks feeling like one repeated punch — the Baki Stage 4 problem.

**2.** **Campaign is 12 stages but the Arcade caps at 10.** `capToArcadeLength` in `data/arcadeCampaignSeries.js` keeps stages 1–9 plus the boss, so these never play: S10 "Effortless Motion", S11 "Still Mind". Fold their content forward and renumber the boss, the way ARC_BAKI was restructured.

> Automatic flags only catch the patterns found in the ARC_BAKI review
> (missing prescriptions, missing tiers, combo droughts, single-strike
> monotony, single-long-round modules, stage-count truncation). Read the
> stage tables above for anything a script can't judge.
