## SETTING — "THE PUMP" (gym x club fusion)

Underground nightclub inside a converted gym: DJ booth towering over the
floor like a pulpit atop stacked weight plates and speaker cabinets;
go-go dancers on squat racks; spin-bike mezzanine with riders pedaling on
the beat; heavy bags hanging among disco balls; dumbbell racks as bar
shelves lined with protein-shaker cocktails; gym flooring painted with a
glowing gold "T". Exterior neon: a dumbbell crossed with a disco ball.

**Vertical staging rule:** the club is built in tall layers — light rig
and DJ pulpit up top, beams raking down through haze, crowd in the
middle band, heroes and floor action at the bottom third. Every frame
should read top-to-bottom, not left-to-right.

## Production notes

- **Aspect:** set 9:16 / 1080x1920 in OpenArt; the prompt says vertical
  but the ratio setting is what actually enforces it.
- **Pixel fidelity:** if output looks smooth/painterly rather than
  pixelated, add `pixel art, visible square pixels, aliased edges,
  no anti-aliasing` and reduce detail settings — smoothness is the most
  common failure mode.
- **Consistency:** generate in order, previous frame as reference; never
  shorten a cast block.
- **If animating:** keep the white blooms as single fast sweeps, never
  repeated strobes (photosensitivity — see 06 §7 for the in-game rules;
  social cuts should follow the same spirit).
- **Cut for social:** shots 4-5-6 (turn it up → beam wipe → camera flip)
  are the hook — lead with them if making a 15s teaser.
