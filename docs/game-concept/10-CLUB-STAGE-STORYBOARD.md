## SETTING — "THE PUMP" (gym x club fusion)

Underground nightclub built inside a converted gym: DJ booth towers over
the floor like a pulpit atop stacked weight plates; go-go dancers on
squat racks; spin-bike mezzanine, riders pedaling on the beat; heavy
bags hanging among disco balls; dumbbell racks as bar shelves with
protein-shaker cocktails; gym-floor dance floor painted with a glowing
gold "T". Exterior neon sign: a dumbbell crossed with a disco ball.
