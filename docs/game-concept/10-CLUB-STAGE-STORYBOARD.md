## 1. TRAINING MODE HOUSE STYLE (mandatory — attach to every frame)

```
TRAINING MODE house style: 16-bit arcade PIXEL ART crossed with 90s
anime. Chunky visible square pixels, aliased stair-step edges, NO
anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients, no painterly or airbrushed
rendering. Thick single-color black outlines around every character and
prop. Flat color fills with exactly two shade steps, plus a hard NEON
VIOLET RIM LIGHT tracing the back edge of every figure — that violet
edge-glow against a near-black ground IS the signature look.

STRICT PALETTE, no other hues: near-black violet ground #080012,
championship gold #FDE047 for every accent, emblem and highlight, neon
violet #A855F7 for energy, glow and rim light, deep warm brown skin
tones, black athletic fabric. Saturated red permitted ONLY on static
neon signage.

Signature details: gold "T" emblem markings on hero gear (chest, gloves,
shoes); heroes have glowing violet eyes; violet aura wisps around fists
during action. Arcade character-select energy — high contrast, figures
POP off a dark background. Sprite quality bar: Streets of Rage 4 x TMNT
Shredder's Revenge. Cyberpunk nightclub built inside a gym.

NEGATIVE: no photorealism, no 3D render, no soft airbrush, no pastel or
muted colors, no anti-aliasing, no lens flare, no motion blur, no
readable text, no watermark.
```

### The signature checklist — every frame must have all six

1. Near-black violet ground (#080012), never grey or blue-black.
2. A hard violet rim light on the back edge of every figure.
3. Gold as the ONLY warm accent in frame.
4. Chunky aliased pixels + thick black outlines.
5. Gold "T" emblems visible on hero gear.
6. Glowing violet eyes on the heroes.

> If a generated frame is missing two or more of these, regenerate — the
> brand read is more important than the composition.

## 2. SETTING — "THE PUMP" (gym x club fusion)

Underground nightclub inside a converted gym: DJ booth towering over the
floor like a pulpit on stacked weight plates and speaker cabinets; go-go
dancers on squat racks; spin-bike mezzanine with riders pedaling on the
beat; heavy bags hanging among disco balls; dumbbell racks as bar shelves
lined with protein-shaker cocktails; gym flooring painted with a glowing
gold "T". Exterior neon: a dumbbell crossed with a disco ball.

**Vertical staging rule:** the club is built in tall layers — light rig
and DJ pulpit up top, gold and violet beams raking down through haze,
silhouetted crowd in the middle band, hero action in the bottom third.
Every frame reads top-to-bottom, not left-to-right.

## 5. Production notes

- **Aspect:** set 9:16 / 1080x1920 in OpenArt — the ratio setting, not
  the prompt, is what enforces it.
- **If output looks smooth or painterly** (the most common failure):
  push `chunky visible square pixels, aliased edges, no anti-aliasing,
  limited palette` harder and lower the detail/realism settings. The
  brand look is high-contrast and FLAT, never rendered.
- **If the palette drifts** (teal, orange, pastels creeping in):
  re-paste the STRICT PALETTE lines — gold and violet must be the only
  chromatic accents on a near-black violet ground.
- **Consistency:** generate in order, previous frame as reference; never
  shorten a cast block.
- **If animating:** keep white blooms as single fast sweeps, never
  repeated strobes (photosensitivity — see 06 §7).
- **Cut for social:** shots 4-5-6 (turn it up → beam wipe → camera flip)
  are the hook — lead with them for a 15s teaser.
