## Music direction (founder-locked)

- Target energy: a **mashup of Natsu Fuji's remix of Rihanna — "Bitch
  Better Have My Money" × Busta Rhymes — "Touch It."**
- Why it's right: "Touch It" is literally structured around DJ commands
  ("turn it up / turn it down") — the song's structure IS the stage's
  mechanic (the DJ escalating the stage modes). BBHMM remix brings the
  menace/strut; Touch It brings the command-and-response escalation.
- ⚠️ Licensing reality: Rihanna + Busta Rhymes (+ the Daft Punk
  "Technologic" sample inside Touch It) is a major-label clearance chain —
  effectively unlicensable at indie budget. Plan: commission an
  **original soundalike** with the same DNA — female-menace hook + male
  command vocals + turn-it-up/turn-it-down dynamic structure the game can
  hook gameplay events onto. (Research pass will confirm costs/options.)

## Stage concept (founder brainstorm, held verbatim)

- Nightclub stage. Lights strobe on/off like **Streets of Rage 3's**
  flashing-light stages.
- The **DJ controls the stage.** While he plays normal, it's a classic
  side-scroller brawl. When he **"turns it up," the stage mode changes.**
- **Three variants:**
  1. **Side-scroller** (base mode) — classic brawling.
  2. **Behind-the-back** — camera swings behind the character;
     Rock'em Sock'em Robots / Punch-Out!! style boxing vs an opponent.
  3. **Top-down** — crowd circles the player; fight via strikes or
     workout moves (button-mash/motion mechanic).
- **The transition:** the crowd all raise their hands, the lights flash
  white, and a stage headlight swings past the camera — that swing IS the
  wipe; when it passes, the new mode is live.
- Tie-in with between-stage systems: the club's pre-stage **commercial**
  is a nightclub promo-style ad (per the escalating gym-ads system in
  02-MAIN-STORY-OUTLINE.md).
